# Vision

## Mission

CaribFlow is an agentic regional opportunity radar designed to help Caribbean stakeholders identify, prioritize and activate high-potential economic opportunities.

## Problem

Economic opportunities often remain unrealized because trade, logistics, policy, climate and market signals are fragmented across institutions, businesses and value chains.

## Solution

CaribFlow continuously monitors regional signals and generates Opportunity Activation Briefs that help stakeholders understand:

- what opportunity exists
- why it matters now
- what barriers exist
- how it can be activated
